PitRelay Privacy Policy and Report Submission Terms

Effective date: October 5, 2026
Version: 1.0

Operator: An individual maintaining PitRelay under the public name catswert (the “Operator”).
Privacy contact: pitrelay@gmail.com

1. Scope and Service Availability

This policy describes the handling of information associated with this website, privacy correspondence and voluntary PitRelay issue reports. The report submission terms govern materials intentionally provided for investigation and improvement of PitRelay.

Report intake is not currently enabled. Report-related provisions apply when an authorized submission form is made available. Intake will remain unavailable until access controls, provider settings and deletion procedures have been verified. Do not send report packages by email or through public GitHub issues, pull requests or comments.

Preparing a report package does not submit it. PitRelay's local operation may retain selected files, reports and checkpoints on the computer running its tools. Selected content returned to a ChatGPT or Codex conversation may be processed by that service. This policy's retention commitments concern information received or controlled by the Operator, not files on your computer or your independently created provider accounts and conversations.

2. Categories and Sources of Information

  • Report information: the issue category, description, optional feedback, submission date and information intentionally included in a report package.
  • Technical materials: selected robot source code, supported telemetry logs, diagnostic files, observations, proposed changes and verification records. Files may contain personal or team identifiers, filenames, timestamps and other identifying information.
  • Conversation excerpts: selected user messages and assistant responses shown to the user. The package format does not request private model reasoning or authorize retrieval of an entire conversation without selection.
  • Correspondence: the sender's email address, message content, relevant attachments and associated message information when the Operator is contacted.
  • Provider information: GitHub, Google and OpenAI may process account, device, network and service-use information under their respective policies, including technical information necessary to deliver their services.

A report ZIP is optional. The report process does not require a team number or personal name. The intended Google Form configuration does not collect email addresses as a response field; the actual setting must be verified before intake opens. Google sign-in is required for file upload, and selecting a file may transfer data before the Submit button is pressed. This process must not be treated as anonymous.

3. Data Minimization and Sanitization

PitRelay attempts to remove identifying details and credentials from selected report materials. Automated redaction and AI-assisted review can miss information. Code, telemetry and writing style may remain identifying. Sanitization does not guarantee anonymity, confidentiality or preservation of the original program's behavior.

Review every file before uploading. Do not submit passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, unrelated personal information or materials you are not authorized to share. Unsupported material may be excluded or may prevent package creation. A partial package must not be represented as complete evidence.

4. Purposes of Processing and AI-Assisted Investigation

The Operator uses reports to assess alleged errors, reproduce problems where possible, investigate product behavior, and improve PitRelay's code, instructions and regression tests. Correspondence is used to answer questions, administer requests and address privacy concerns. Reports may be screened and closed without investigation if irrelevant, unusable or outside the product's scope.

The Operator periodically uses OpenAI Codex to investigate selected reports. Package contents, associated prompts, tool results and generated findings may be processed by OpenAI's hosted models even when files and deterministic tools are on the Operator's computer. AI findings are reviewed before changes are accepted.

The Operator does not use submitted materials to train or fine-tune AI model weights, sell them, use them for advertising, or publish report packages or private excerpts. AI investigation is permitted only after verifying that eligible report-content use for OpenAI model improvement is disabled, including through the applicable separate Codex environment control. Report-bearing interactions must not be submitted as provider feedback that could authorize training use. These controls do not prevent processing required to provide the service or override provider retention obligations.

General software improvements and independently authored synthetic tests may be retained and published, provided they do not retain submitted private code, telemetry, identifying information or other protected report content. Any future model-weight training program would require a separate notice and express opt-in; this policy does not authorize it.

5. Recipients and Access

The Operator is the sole authorized human reviewer designated by PitRelay. Google Forms and Drive provide report intake and storage when enabled; Google's email service processes privacy correspondence. OpenAI provides Codex-assisted investigation. These providers process information under their applicable terms, privacy policies and account controls. GitHub hosts this public website and its policy source files; it is not the report repository.

Sole designated human review does not exclude necessary service-provider processing, authorized administrative access or disclosure required by applicable law or valid legal process. Access to report storage is restricted to the stated purposes. No claim of absolute security, exclusive domestic processing or immediate deletion from provider-controlled systems is made.

6. Retention and Deletion

Reports accepted through enabled intake, associated feedback and identifiable working copies controlled by the Operator are deleted within 90 calendar days of submission, and earlier when no longer necessary. Copying, downloading, extracting or reprocessing a report does not restart that period. Privacy correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to address the inquiry or a specific legal obligation, and is deleted when no longer needed for those purposes.

The report deadline covers Form responses, Drive uploads, linked spreadsheets, downloaded and extracted files, working copies, owner-controlled trash and backups, and report-bearing AI tasks, conversations, attachments and findings. Deletion requires removal of controlled copies and use of available provider deletion processes; archiving alone is insufficient. Report content is not retained indefinitely in source-control history or unmanaged backups.

Where a specific legal obligation requires longer retention of particular information, its use is restricted to that obligation and it is deleted when the obligation ends. An unresolved issue or possible future research does not extend the deadline. Google and OpenAI may retain service-level deletion, recovery, security, legal or backup records under their own policies. The Operator cannot guarantee immediate removal from those independent systems or erase copies retained by the submitter.

7. Access, Correction and Deletion Requests

Send privacy questions and requests to review, correct or delete information to pitrelay@gmail.com. Include the submission date and report reference or brief description, if available. Do not resend the ZIP or provide unnecessary sensitive information. Limited additional information may be requested to locate the record and verify that the requester is entitled to act on it.

For reports accepted through enabled intake, verified deletion requests for copies controlled by the Operator are completed within 30 calendar days, or sooner where applicable law requires. A request does not extend an existing 90-day retention deadline. Requests concerning other privacy correspondence are handled in accordance with applicable law. If a record cannot be located or a specific legal obligation prevents full deletion, the limitation will be explained. Requests to stop further optional investigation may be made through the same contact. Nothing in these terms limits rights available under applicable law.

8. Submission Authorization and Ownership

Submission is voluntary and is not required to use PitRelay. By choosing to submit a report through the authorized form and accepting its submission acknowledgement, the submitter confirms that they have authority to provide the selected materials and authorizes the Operator and the named service providers to copy and process them for the purposes and within the retention limits stated here.

Submission does not transfer ownership of code, logs or other materials, grant a right to publish private submitted content, or authorize model-weight training. The submitter should obtain any required permission from the team, rights holder or affected individual before submission. A report does not guarantee investigation, correction or an individual response and is not an urgent match-support service.

9. Information Concerning Children

The optional report service is not directed to children under 13 and must not be used to submit personal information about a child under 13. A parent or adult mentor may assist a student with an authorized submission. If the Operator learns that information was collected without legally required authorization, its use will stop and appropriate deletion and compliance steps will be taken. This provision does not replace safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Website Hosting and Tracking

This static website includes no PitRelay analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms or file-upload functions. It does not change its behavior in response to a browser's Do Not Track signal. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security and may process other technical request information in providing hosting. Linked third-party services may collect or associate account and usage information with activity on their other services under their own policies. The Operator does not control those independent practices.

11. Policy Amendments

Material changes will be identified in a notice on this page with an updated effective date and version before they take effect. Where a usable contact address is held, affected submitters will also be notified directly when required. Any required fresh permission will be obtained before applying materially different purposes or recipients to existing information. A later amendment does not retroactively authorize public release of submitted content or model-weight training.
